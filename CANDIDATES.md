# Candidates & pairwise tournament

Competition: `u-donrulezz-the-absurd-clause-contest-035cc0f1` ($2, deadline 2026-11-26T20:25Z).
Task: one original English sentence, max 200 chars, an absurd clause that could hide
deep inside a terms-of-service agreement.

Judge brief criteria, in weight order:
1. Is an absurd clause that could hide deep inside a ToS (off-genre loses).
2. Funny on its own; joke lands on one reading, no outside setup.
3. Sounds like real legal boilerplate until the absurdity hits; the joke comes
   from the register as much as from the words.
4. Original and specific; a concrete absurd image, not a vague shrug at the genre.
5. Tight and well written; one clean sentence, no wasted words, no explaining
   its own joke.

A clause that only works once the reader already knows it is a joke is weaker
than one that ambushes them. Pairwise comparisons apply the criteria in weight
order: the first criterion where two candidates differ decides, unless a later
criterion differs by much more.
